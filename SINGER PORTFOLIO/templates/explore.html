Sujata Gurav Kammar

Hindustani Classical Vocalist | Kirana Gharana

Vidushi Smt. Sujata Gurav Kammar is an accomplished Hindustani classical vocalist associated with the Kirana Gharana tradition. She was born on 19 November 1973 into a family with a strong musical heritage. She received her initial musical training from her father and guru, Late Pandit Sangameshwar Gurav, a respected musician of the Kirana Gharana tradition. She later continued her musical training under her brother, Pandit Kaivalya Kumar Gurav. Sujata Gurav Kammar is known for her melodious voice, expressive rendition of ragas, intricate taans and murkis, and strong rhythmic understanding. Along with Hindustani classical music, she has also performed Vachana, Dasavani, Abhang, Thumri and Natya Sangeet, demonstrating her versatility as a vocalist.
